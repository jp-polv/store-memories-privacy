Store Memories – Data and Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of information stored in Store Memories, including individual memories and photos or your entire account and its associated data.

You are in control of your data. You may request deletion of selected content or deletion of your complete Store Memories account.

What you can ask us to delete

Specific memories and photos

You may request deletion of one or more stored memories and their associated photos. A memory may include its title, story text, location text, date, category, and uploaded photos.

Your full account and associated data

You may request deletion of your Store Memories account, Firebase Authentication account information, Firestore user profile and memory records, and associated photos stored in Supabase Storage.

How to submit a deletion request

  1. Email jpolvorosa86@gmail.com.
  2. Include the email address associated with your Store Memories account.
  3. State whether you want specific memories or photos deleted, or your full account and associated data deleted. If requesting specific deletion, clearly identify the relevant memory or content.
Email a deletion request

Identity verification

To protect your account and personal information, we may need to verify that you are the account owner before processing a deletion request. We may reply to the email address associated with your Store Memories account or ask for other information reasonably needed to confirm your identity. Please do not send your password.

What happens after your request

Once your identity and request are confirmed, we will delete the requested data unless limited retention is reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of agreements. Deletion from active systems may not be immediate, and limited copies may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or logs according to their retention processes.

PDF files that you previously exported and saved to your own device are not removed by deleting server-stored data. You can delete those local files using the app or your device's file controls.

More information

For details about how Store Memories handles personal information, read the Store Memories Privacy Policy.
