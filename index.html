Store Memories Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Store Memories (Android package com.jppolv.storememories) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

In brief: Store Memories uses your information to provide account, memory, photo, and PDF-export features. The app does not currently show third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information. Firebase and Supabase process data on our behalf to provide the app's services.

1. Information we collect

We collect information that you provide, information needed to operate the app, and limited technical information processed by our service providers.

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your full name and email address. Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in credentials and authentication information. Depending on how you use the app and service, this may include your password, authentication identifiers, IP address, user-agent information, and related security or account-management data. We do not have access to your password in readable form.

User-created memory content

We collect and store the content you choose to add to a memory, including its title, story or description, category, memory date, favourite status, and related record information such as creation and update times.

Photos and media

When you choose a photo from your device or take a new photo, the app processes and uploads that selected image so it can be saved with your memory. The app may request camera or photo access when you use these features. Photos remain grouped with the memory to which you add them. Image files are stored using Supabase Storage, while the corresponding storage paths are associated with your memory record in Cloud Firestore.

Location information entered by you

A memory may include a location that you type or otherwise choose to enter. This field stores the location text you provide. It is not used for advertising. Store Memories does not use this field to continuously track your device's location.

PDF and story exports

When you create a story or PDF export, the app processes the memories and photos you select to generate the document. Generated PDFs may be saved on your device. If you choose to share or save a PDF through another app or service, that recipient or service will handle the file under its own privacy terms.

2. Firebase and Supabase services

Firebase

Store Memories uses Google Firebase Authentication to create and manage accounts and Cloud Firestore to store user profiles and memory data. Google may process information as our service provider to operate, secure, maintain, and support these services. Processing and storage locations may vary by Firebase service and configuration. More information is available in Firebase's privacy and security documentation.

Supabase Storage

Store Memories uses Supabase Storage to store uploaded photos. Supabase may process photo files, storage object information, and technical data needed to upload, retrieve, secure, and delete those files. Uploaded photos are stored in a private bucket and access is controlled through authentication and storage access policies. Private storage reduces public access but cannot guarantee that unauthorised access will never occur. More information is available in Supabase's privacy information and its Storage access-control documentation.

3. How we use information

We use information described in this policy to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage user accounts;
  • save, organise, display, edit, and delete memories;
  • upload, retrieve, display, and delete memory photos;
  • generate, save, open, and share PDFs at your request;
  • maintain the app's functionality and respond to support requests;
  • protect accounts, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your memory content or photos to serve third-party advertisements. The app does not currently display third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Data storage and security

Account and memory information is stored using Firebase services, and uploaded photos are stored using Supabase Storage. These providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their terms and applicable law.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including authenticated access, Firestore security rules, private photo storage, and storage access policies. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials private.

5. Data sharing

We may disclose or make information available in these situations:

  • Service providers: Google Firebase and Supabase process data on our behalf to provide authentication, database, and photo-storage services.
  • At your direction: information is shared when you choose to export, download, or share a PDF or other content.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed if reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or a valid government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users, Store Memories, or others.

We do not share personal information with third-party advertisers because the app does not currently use third-party advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

We generally retain account information and user-created content while your account is active or for as long as needed to provide the app. When you delete a memory, the app is designed to delete its memory record and associated uploaded photos. Locally saved PDF exports remain on your device until you delete them through the app or your device's file controls.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at jpolvorosa86@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Once verified, we will delete or anonymise information associated with your account unless retention is reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of agreements.

Deletion from active systems may not be immediate. Limited copies may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or logs and are removed or overwritten according to the providers' retention processes.

7. Children's privacy

Store Memories is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required by applicable law to consent to the processing of personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate deletion steps.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can edit or delete memories and photos through the app's available controls. You may also contact us to make a privacy request.

We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and account before acting on a request. Some rights may be limited by applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent before a material change takes effect.
